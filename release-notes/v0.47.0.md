## Recipe images

* The suggestion and week tiles and the taste profile now show the **preview image of the recipe page**.
* Images load straight from the recipe site (nothing is downloaded or stored on your Home Assistant). Only https images are used.
* **No broken images:** an image stays invisible until it has loaded; if it fails or is only a tiny placeholder it is removed and the tile looks as before. Failed images are remembered and not retried on every update.
* Turn images off with `images: false` in the card config.
