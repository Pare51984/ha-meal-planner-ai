## One tab for taste profile and recipes

* The **Recipes** tab is merged into **Taste profile**. It shows the free text on top and below it all rated recipes in the groups liked (7–10), neutral (5–6) and disliked (1–4), with search, sorting, stars, notes and the recently cooked dishes. Changing the stars moves a recipe to the matching group, so everyone sees right away what the AI takes from it.
* **Taste profile is visible to every user.** Everyone can rate, remove ratings and write notes. The free text can be read by everyone but only changed by administrators; deleting recipes stays admin only.
* The card now has up to three tabs: Meal plan, Taste profile, Settings (admins). The separate `meal-planner-memory-card` shows the same view.
