## Staples fix

* Ingredients like "weißer Pfeffer" or "frisch gemahlener Pfeffer" are now recognised as staples when "Pfeffer" is in your staples list (before only single words like "Meersalz" were). The last word counts, e.g. "brauner Zucker" for "Zucker".
* Names the AI cleans up for the shopping list are checked against the staples again, so "Pfeffer, weiß" no longer slips through.
