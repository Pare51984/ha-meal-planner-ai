## New

* **More suggestions** button in the card: adds 5 more dishes to choose from, everything you already have stays. Typical flow: set a dish to "not after all", tap *More suggestions*, pick one of the new dishes.
  * Before the selection deadline the dishes are added to the coming week's suggestions.
  * During a running week they appear under "To choose for this week" and only go on the shopping list once you pick a shopping trip. Need one dish more than planned? Same button.
  * With no suggestions and no running week the button reads *Plan suggestions now* and plans the coming week right away (tap twice).
* **Swap** on every dish that is not cooked yet: replaces it with another dish (tap twice). The shopping trip stays, the shopping list is adjusted.
* New actions `meal_planner_ai.more_proposals` (count 1–10, optional notes) and `meal_planner_ai.swap_dish`.
