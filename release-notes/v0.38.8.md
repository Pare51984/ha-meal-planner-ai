## Fixes

* Shopping list: entries that no longer exist on the list (for example because the list app assigned new IDs) no longer cause a row of "Unable to find to-do list item" errors. Removing and merging re-read the list and retry once, then skip quietly.
* Shopping list sync handles each dish on its own, so one failing dish no longer stops the others from being added.
* AI calls now time out after 4 minutes instead of blocking all buttons until a restart.

## Card

* "Not after all" removes the dish right away. An "Undo" bar shows for 8 seconds at the bottom.
* While the AI is searching, "More suggestions" and "Swap" show the running state on every device and after reloading. A second tap no longer shows an error.

## Install

* Releases now come with `meal_planner_ai.zip`. HACS installs from it, and GitHub counts the downloads.
