## 10-star ratings

* Ratings now use **10 stars** instead of 5, for finer feedback. The card shows the value next to the stars (e.g. 7/10).
* **Existing ratings are converted once** on the first start after the update: every rating is doubled (4 of 5 becomes 8 of 10, 1 becomes 2). Recipe history and the dishes of the stored weeks are converted, nothing is lost.
* Liked now means 7–10 stars, disliked 1–4 stars (before: 4–5 and 1–2). The taste profile, the AI prompt and the plan check use the new limits.

## Please check your automations

* The actions `mark_cooked`, `update_history`, `list_recipes` (`min_rating`) and the import actions now take ratings from 1 to 10. Values from 1 to 5 are still accepted but are read on the new scale, so an automation that sends `rating: 4` now means 4 of 10. Double the values in your own automations or scripts.
