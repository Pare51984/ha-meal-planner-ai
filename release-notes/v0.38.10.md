## Fixes

* Home Assistant's list of LLM APIs (used when configuring a conversation agent) no longer breaks with "object has no attribute 'requires_admin'".

## Card

* Suggestions that were not picked now have a **Remove** button, both in the coming week's suggestions and in the "to choose for this week" list (dishes added via "More suggestions"). Removing can be undone from the bar at the bottom.
* "Remove remaining suggestions" clears all unpicked dishes of the running week at once (tap twice to confirm).
